# SpeddyProp Admin Portal - Easy Client Guide
## Complete Guide: All Features & How They Work

---

## 1. Quick Overview

The **SpeddyProp Admin Portal** is the central control panel for managing your entire real estate website and mobile apps. From this panel, you can manage buyers, approve agents, manage property listings, configure subscription plans, import properties from other websites, tune the AI assistant, and view business analytics.

---

## 2. Main Sections & How They Work

---

### 🔑 1. Login & Security

**What it is:** The secure entry point for administrators to access the portal.

**✨ Key Features:**
* Secure login with Email and Password.
* "Forgot Password" with a 3-step verification code (OTP) sent to your email.
* Automatic session renewal so you don't get abruptly logged out while working.
* Automatic logout on inactivity to protect your business data.

**🔄 How it Works:**
1. Enter your Admin Email and Password, then click **Login**.
2. If you forgot your password:
   * Click **Forgot Password**.
   * Enter your registered email address.
   * Enter the 6-digit OTP code received in your inbox.
   * Type your new password and submit.

---

### 📊 2. Dashboard (Live Business Overview)

**What it is:** The homepage that displays the overall health and real-time statistics of your real estate business.

**✨ Key Features:**
* **Total Counts:** See total registered Users, Active Realtors, Listed Properties, and New Inquiries at a glance.
* **Search Trends:** See which cities/neighborhoods buyers are searching for the most.
* **Unmet Demand Gaps:** Highlights areas where buyers are searching for homes, but no listings are available yet (great for targeting new sellers).
* **Lead Conversion Funnel:** Visual chart showing how many visitors turn into real buyer inquiries.
* **Recent Activity Feed:** Shows the last 5 actions performed on the platform.

**🔄 How it Works:**
1. Log into the panel — the Dashboard opens automatically.
2. Select a time filter (**Last 7 Days**, **30 Days**, **90 Days**, or **1 Year**) to update charts.
3. Click on any summary card to quickly jump to that specific management page.

---

### 👥 3. User Management (Buyers & Clients)

**What it is:** A directory of all regular users and property buyers registered on your website.

**✨ Key Features:**
* Full list of registered users with search by name, email, or phone number.
* Filter users by status: **Active**, **Suspended**, or **Banned**.
* View each user's saved favorite properties and submitted inquiries.
* Direct Password Reset button to help users who are locked out.
* Add new user profiles manually.
* One-click account suspension or deletion for spam accounts.

**🔄 How it Works:**
1. **Search a User:** Type the user's name, email, or phone into the search bar.
2. **View User Profile:** Click the **Eye icon** to view their contact info, saved properties, and inquiry history.
3. **Reset Password:** Click the **Key icon** to assign a temporary or new password for the user.
4. **Block / Delete:** Click the **Status toggle** to suspend the user, or the **Trash icon** to delete them.

---

### 🏢 4. Realtor Management (Agents & Brokers)

**What it is:** The hub for reviewing, approving, and managing real estate agents who list properties on your platform.

**✨ Key Features:**
* **Agent Approval Pipeline:** Separate tabs for **Pending**, **Approved**, and **Rejected** agent applications.
* **Document Verification:** Preview submitted Government IDs, Real Estate Licenses, and Agency affiliation letters.
* **Agent Performance:** View how many active listings each agent has and how many buyer leads they received.
* **Tier Assignment:** Assign or change the subscription plan for any agent.

**🔄 How it Works:**
1. When a new agent registers on your website, they appear under the **Pending** tab.
2. Click on the agent's name to open their profile.
3. Review their submitted license and ID documents.
4. Click **Approve** to give them a verified badge and allow them to post listings, or click **Reject** with an explanation.

---

### 🏛️ 5. Agency Management (Real Estate Companies)

**What it is:** Manage real estate brokerages, development companies, and agencies that have multiple agents under them.

**✨ Key Features:**
* Manage company profiles (Company Name, Logo, Address, Tax ID, Phone, Website).
* View all individual agents working under a specific agency.
* Toggle verified status badge for trusted agencies.
* Switch between **Grid Cards** view and **Table List** view.

**🔄 How it Works:**
1. Click **+ Add Agency** to create a new brokerage profile with their logo and details.
2. Click **View Agents** on any agency card to see the full list of agents affiliated with them.
3. Click **Edit** to update agency contact information or their assigned business plan.

---

### 👑 6. Tier Management (Subscription Plans & Pricing)

**What it is:** Set up and manage the pricing plans that realtors and agencies purchase to list properties.

**✨ Key Features:**
* Switch between **Monthly** and **Yearly** billing package views.
* View plan pricing, features, and quotas for each tier (e.g., Free, Silver, Gold, Platinum).
* See limits for each plan:
  * Maximum number of property listings allowed.
  * Number of "Featured / Highlighted" listings.
  * AI search query limits.
  * Priority lead routing and direct customer support.

**🔄 How it Works:**
1. Navigate to **Tier Management** from the sidebar.
2. Toggle between **Monthly** and **Yearly** tabs to review pricing structures.
3. Click on any plan card to view all the included feature limits and quotas.

---

### 🏡 7. Property Management (Listings & Deduplication)

**What it is:** The complete inventory of all houses, apartments, villas, and commercial properties on your platform.

**✨ Key Features:**
* **Comprehensive Search & Filters:** Filter properties by Sale / Rent, Price Range, Location, Bedrooms, Property Type, and Status (Active / Pending / Sold).
* **Grid & List Views:** View properties as visual cards with photos or as a compact table.
* **Add & Edit Listings:** Upload multiple photos, set price, add description, pick amenities (pool, parking, security), set map location, and assign an agent.
* **Duplicate Detection & Merge:** Automatically detects when the same property is listed multiple times (or scraped from external portals) and allows you to merge them into one clean listing.

**🔄 How it Works:**
1. **Add a Property:** Click **+ Add Property**, fill in the price, location, bedrooms, upload images, select the assigned agent, and click **Save**.
2. **Edit a Listing:** Click the **Edit icon** on any property to change details or mark it as **Sold** or **Rented**.
3. **Merge Duplicates:** If a duplicate warning appears on two listings, click **Merge**, choose which one to keep as the primary listing, and confirm.

---

### ⚡ 8. Portal & API Sync (External Feeds & Scrapers)

**What it is:** Automatically imports and synchronizes property listings from external portals (like SuperCasas, Corotos, or MLS feeds).

**✨ Key Features:**
* Connect and manage multiple external real estate feeds.
* Set automatic sync schedules (e.g., **Every Hour**, **Daily**, or **Weekly**).
* **Live Sync Button:** Trigger a real-time manual import whenever you want fresh listings.
* **Sync Terminal Logs:** See how many new properties were imported, how many duplicates were ignored, and if any errors occurred.

**🔄 How it Works:**
1. Go to **Portal & API Sync**.
2. To run an immediate update, click **Start Manual Sync**.
3. Watch the live progress bar and terminal log showing imported properties in real time.
4. View previous sync logs with timestamps and status indicators.

---

### 🤖 9. AI Management (Smart Search & Description Writer)

**What it is:** Configure the AI assistant that powers smart property search for buyers and generates descriptions for realtors.

**✨ Key Features:**
* **AI Search Tuning:** Control how the AI understands natural language search (e.g., if a buyer types *"modern 2-bed apartment near the beach under $200k"*).
* **Location Mapping:** Teach the AI to match local neighborhood nicknames to official city areas.
* **Marketing Copywriter:** Powers the tool that automatically writes attractive listing descriptions for agents.
* **Live AI Sandbox:** Test search queries directly in the admin panel to see how the AI responds before making it live.

**🔄 How it Works:**
1. Open **AI Management**.
2. Choose the AI tab you want to configure: **Search Assistant** or **Marketing Copywriter**.
3. Adjust the system instructions or prompt.
4. Type a test question into the **Sandbox box** (e.g., *"Find a villa in Punta Cana with a private pool"*) and click **Run Test** to preview the AI's response.
5. Click **Save Configuration**.

---

### 📈 10. Reports & Analytics

**What it is:** Business reports, growth graphs, and performance charts that you can export and share with stakeholders.

**✨ Key Features:**
* **Lead Acquisition Graphs:** Track how many buyer inquiries you receive daily, weekly, and monthly.
* **Top Performing Realtors:** Leaderboard showing which agents get the most views and inquiries.
* **Most Popular Locations:** Heatmap ranking the most searched cities and property types.
* **1-Click Export:** Download any report as an **Excel Spreadsheet (.xlsx)**, **CSV file**, or **PDF Document**.

**🔄 How it Works:**
1. Navigate to **Reports & Analytics**.
2. Select your desired date range and report category.
3. Review the visual charts and tables on screen.
4. Click **Export Excel**, **Export CSV**, or **Export PDF** at the top right to download the file to your computer.

---

### 🔔 11. Notifications & Lead Alerts

**What it is:** The top-bar notification bell that instantly alerts you when important events happen.

**✨ Key Features:**
* Automatically checks for new updates every 30 seconds.
* Alerts you about:
  * New buyer property inquiries.
  * New realtor registration applications waiting for approval.
  * Portal sync completions or errors.
* Click any notification to jump directly to that specific lead or application.
* **Mark All as Read** and **Delete** controls.

**🔄 How it Works:**
1. When a new inquiry arrives, a red badge counter appears on the **Bell icon** in the top header.
2. Click the bell to open the dropdown list.
3. Click any inquiry to open its details, or click **Mark All as Read** to clear the badge.

---

### 📜 12. Audit Logs (Security & Activity Trail)

**What it is:** A secure history log that records every single action taken inside the admin panel.

**✨ Key Features:**
* Records who did what, when, and from which IP address.
* Tracks actions such as: *User Created*, *Property Edited*, *Realtor Approved*, *Listing Deleted*, *Password Changed*.
* Filter logs by Action Type (**Create**, **Edit**, **Delete**) or search by keyword.

**🔄 How it Works:**
1. Open **Audit & System Logs** from the sidebar.
2. Use the search bar or filter dropdowns to find a specific action or administrator.
3. View the exact timestamp and details of the change.

---

### ⚙️ 13. System Settings & Admin Profile

**What it is:** Configure your personal admin account and global platform preferences.

**✨ Key Features:**
* **Admin Profile (`/profile`):** Update your admin name, email, phone number, and upload a new profile avatar.
* **Change Password (`/change-password`):** Securely update your login password.
* **Notification Preferences (`/settings`):** Choose which alerts you want to receive via Email or in-app sounds.
* **System Backups:** Create manual database backups and restore them if needed.

**🔄 How it Works:**
1. Click your profile avatar at the top right or bottom left of the sidebar.
2. Select **Profile** to update your name/photo, or **Change Password** to update your password.
3. Go to **System Settings** to toggle email alert preferences on or off with simple switches.

---

### 📄 14. Terms & Conditions (`/terms-edit` & `/terms`)

**What it is:** Manage and publish the legal Terms and Conditions for SpeddyProp in both English and Spanish.

**✨ Key Features:**
* View public document page in English (`/terms?language=english`) and Spanish (`/terms?language=spanish`).
* Full CKEditor 5 WYSIWYG editor for bold, italics, numbered/bullet lists, headings, and tables.
* Live word count, character count, and unsaved changes protection.

---

### 🛡️ 15. Privacy Policy (`/privacy-policy-edit` & `/privacy-policy`)

**What it is:** Manage and publish the official Privacy & Security Policy for SpeddyProp in both English and Spanish.

**✨ Key Features:**
* View public document page in English (`/privacy-policy?language=english`) and Spanish (`/privacy-policy?language=spanish`).
* Full CKEditor 5 rich-text editor for editing policy clauses.
* Instant updates synchronized with web and mobile applications via REST API.
